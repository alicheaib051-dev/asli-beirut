names them both.

The Duo Chicken Combo is off this screen.

The dessert adds 6 AED to every bowl, which is the point of the line above the
cards on the still board. The meat and kafta bowls read 36 for a while against
combos of 43, which made the dessert 7 on those two; they are back at 37, so it
is 6 on all four again. The combo signs are the original artwork, untouched —
it is the meat bowl card that was repainted, on main's own render.
