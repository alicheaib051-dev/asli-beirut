Silicon Oasis cut — the two branches run the same films, and the closing card
names them both.

WORTH KNOWING: the dessert adds 6 AED to the two chicken bowls and 7 AED to the
meat and the kafta. That is why there is no single "add dessert for 6 AED" line
above the cards any more — it would only be true of half of them. Each card
carries its own two prices instead. If the meat and kafta combos should come
down to 42, say so and both the film and the board are reprinted in a few
minutes.
