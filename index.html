Our Story

The real thing, asli.

"Asli" means original — and that's a promise. Our recipes come straight from Beiruti family kitchens: hummus whipped fresh every morning, kibbeh shaped by hand, shawarma stacked and slow-roasted daily, and arayes sealed over live coals.

From our first kitchen in Dubai Silicon Oasis to our home at Dubai Hills Mall, everything is cooked over charcoal and seasoned with the generosity Lebanon is famous for.

Hikayetna — Our Story

From Beirut,
with love & garlic.

حكايتنا — من بيروت إلى دبي

Every restaurant has a menu. Ours has a memory — of Sunday tables in Beirut, of dough rising before sunrise, of charcoal that never goes cold.

The Roots

Recipes from Beirut, never a restaurant

Long before Asli existed, these were family recipes — kibbeh shaped by hand the way our grandmothers taught, spices ground fresh, charcoal always hot. We never ran a restaurant in Lebanon. This wasn't something we opened and grew from there. These were home flavours, guarded and perfected for generations, waiting for the right moment to share them with the world.

الأكل هو المحبة
2022

Flying the flavours to Dubai — two homes, simultaneously

The moment came. We took those guarded family recipes — the ones that never had a storefront in Lebanon — and we brought them to Dubai. Not one location. Both. Dubai Silicon Oasis and Dubai Hills Mall opened on the same day, carrying the same soul, the same standards, zero compromises. We didn't grow from a single branch. We arrived fully formed, with charcoal that never stops glowing and a menu built on generations of home cooking. "Asli" meant the original. It still does.

حملنا النكهات من بيروت إلى دبي
Today

Same flavours, same soul, two homes

The craft hasn't changed. The charcoal is lit before the doors open. Every skewer is seasoned the Beiruti way we learned before we ever thought of opening a door. From the charcoal that never cools to the plates on your table — we keep the same standards that kept those recipes alive in a home kitchen for generations. Two locations. One heartbeat. Asli, always.
